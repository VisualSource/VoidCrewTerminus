Upgrade Forge

Module progression L3 → L10 shipped MP partly verified

The mod's flagship system: a new installable Forge module that consumes relics to raise other modules past their vanilla level cap, with tiered relics, rolled perks, cursed drawbacks and sector escalation. All progression is per-run. Eight phases built; Phase 8 multiplayer sync is only partly verified in-game.

RFC Upgrade Forge — RFC The proposal: why extend module levels at all, what the crew does with the Forge, and what stays out of scope. docs/upgrade-forge-rfc.html Design · authoritative Upgrade Forge — System Design The mod-side overlay, relic tiers, perk pools, Forge Meter, Sector Escalation and the phase breakdown. The reference test plans point back to. docs/upgrade-forge-design.html Authoring guide Upgrade Forge — Prefab Authoring The named-transform contract between UpgradeForgeModuleCell.prefab and the runtime wiring, for editing the prefab in Unity. docs/upgrade-forge-prefab-authoring.html

Leech Carrier

EVA-forcing combat encounter design complete not built

A deep-sector enemy that fires Leech Missiles; on impact they deploy parasites onto the hull that degrade nearby modules until a crewmember EVAs out and burns them off with the multi-tool. Gated behind DifficultyScalar ≥ 2 from the Forge's escalation system. Implementation was never authorized.

RFC Leech Carrier Encounter — RFC The encounter as players meet it: the missile, the 1–3 Leeches, the hold-to-remove EVA interaction, and the difficulty gate. docs/leech-rfc.html Design Leech Carrier Encounter — System Design Technical companion: how a vanilla enemy is patched to carry the weapon, how Leeches attach and network, and how it extends Sector Escalation. docs/leech-design.html

Aux Fighter

Single-seat craft carried by the ship design only not scheduled

A Fighter Bay module that clamps a one-seat craft to the hull's exterior. Module power is the docking mechanism, which makes launch a two-crew job and a power outage an emergency. Larger than the Forge; captured while fresh rather than queued.

RFC Aux Fighter & Fighter Bay — RFC The design in player terms: launch and recovery, power as the latch, the narrow anti-fighter combat role, and what the jump costs a pilot left outside. docs/aux-fighter-rfc.html Design Aux Fighter — System Design The five-state latch machine, the SpaceCraft and cockpit seat, PhotonView ownership transfer, patch surfaces and the phased build. docs/aux-fighter-design.html
